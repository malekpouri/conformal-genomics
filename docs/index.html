How it works

Marginal coverage (split-conformal)

Any predictor is wrapped for finite-sample, distribution-free coverage under exchangeability:

$$1-\alpha \le \Pr(y\in C(x)) \le 1-\alpha+\tfrac{1}{n+1}.$$

A multi-objective $\infty$-norm score covers both objectives with one quantile (no union bound).

cfBH selective FAR control

Scalar target $T(y)=\min\{(y_{\mathrm{eff}}-\tau_{\mathrm{eff}})/s_{\mathrm{eff}},\,(\log(1{+}\tau_{\mathrm{off}})-\log(1{+}y_{\mathrm{off}}))/s_{\mathrm{off}}\}$, monotone score $V(x,t)=t-\hat T(x)$, full-calibration p-values + Benjamini–Hochberg:

$$p_j=\frac{1+\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathbb{1}\{V(X_i,T_i)\le V(X_j,0)\}}{n+1}.$$

Controls the False Acceptance Rate (an FDR-type criterion) at $\mathrm{FAR}\le q$ in finite samples.

Supporting evidence

Real generator + coverage at scale

  • • Char-level autoregressive GRU trained on 55,603 real spacers → 100% novel, 100% unique candidates.
  • • Marginal coverage nominal at 55k scale (0.899–0.900 at nominal 0.90).
  • • Coverage on the efficacy task is unaffected by the grouping key (0.903 grouped vs 0.901 random); the leakage reported in CORRECTION.md is specific to the off-target benchmark file.

Honest limits (what does not work)

Efficacy noise floor

Indel-efficacy is intrinsically hard: even at 55k the efficacy oracle tops out at Spearman ≤ 0.78, below the ≳ 0.9 fidelity cfBH needs. Efficacy-only and joint selection power are ≈ 0 — a property of the assay, not of conformal prediction. More data does not help.

Coverage does not transfer — but 50 labels fix it

A wild-type-calibrated predictor covers at 0.01–0.51 across engineered nucleases and cell lines, at nominal 0.90. Recalibrating on 50 target-domain labels restores 0.899–0.906 in every panel — that, not covariate-shift reweighting, is the remedy the data support: eSpCas9 and SpCas9-HF1 share 93.7% and 95.4% of their guides with the calibration set, so there is no shift to reweight. No cohort carries both validated efficacy and validated off-target, so the multi-objective guarantee is validated per objective, never jointly.

Deployment regime & the fail-safe property

Off-target burden is an alignment statistic: the free count of enumerated near-match sites tracks validated per-guide burden at ρ = 0.964 [0.79, 1.00], where the trained 181-feature oracle reaches ρ = 0.018 once the guide is unseen. From the 20-nt sequence alone ρ ≈ 0. In every regime cfBH fails safe — it selects nothing rather than falsely accepting — which at these cohort sizes is all it does.

What the certificate is worth — at matched yield

Corrected: the tuned threshold's violation gap is a yield gap. At its own operating point it accepts about twice as much and violates FAR ≤ q on 37–47% of splits; constrained to accept exactly what cfBH accepted, its FAR is 0.001 — identical to cfBH's — and its violation rate falls to 0.01. The certificate picks a conservative operating point automatically; it does not make a given accepted set safer.
